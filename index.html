Hi, I’m Kyle!

I study math, computer science, and creative writing at Stanford University.

I do research on decentralized multi-agent systems at the Stanford AI Lab under Azalia Mirhoseini. Before that, I worked on AI agents at Aven, built evaluations at YC-backed Paradigm, and red-teamed frontier models at Scale AI. I was also a Research Fellow at Contrary and a Summer Fellow at Dorm Room Fund.

I enjoy creative writing and have won national awards for my flash fiction and poetry. I play piano for the Stanford Symphony Orchestra, a two-piano chamber duo, and a band called Craig. I love improvising and composing (here is a paraphrase for two organs on John Adams’ Hallelujah Junction that I co-wrote). I directed and scored a documentary about my piano teacher, which won a YoungArts award.

In my free time, I love chasing sunsets, ranking new restaurants on Beli, and cooking for my friends and family. I’m on a quest to find the best carbonara in the Bay Area.

A very long time ago, I wrote and published five novels. There’s a minor planet named after me.
